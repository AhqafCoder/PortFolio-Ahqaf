# Adding Project Images to Your Portfolio

To complete your portfolio setup, you need to add project screenshots to the `public/projects/` folder. 

## Required Images:

Add the following images to `public/projects/` directory:

1. **opengeek-community.png** - Screenshot of https://community.opengeek.in/
2. **invertisprep.png** - Screenshot of https://www.invertisprep.in/
3. **healing-website.png** - Screenshot of https://showershealing.com/
4. **hms.png** - Screenshot or mockup of your Hostel Management System
5. **opengeek-main.png** - Screenshot of https://www.opengeek.in/
6. **ezcode.png** - Screenshot of https://ezcode-xi.vercel.app/
7. **cweb.png** - Screenshot of https://cweb-7pbz.onrender.com/
8. **docsmart.png** - Mockup or design of your DocSmart system
9. **indipixel.png** - Screenshot of https://www.indipixel.online/

## Image Specifications:
- **Format**: PNG or JPG
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (recommended)
- **Size**: Approximately 1200x675 pixels
- **File Size**: Keep under 500KB for optimal loading

## How to Add:
1. Take screenshots of your live projects
2. Resize them to the recommended dimensions
3. Save them with the exact names listed above
4. Place them in the `public/projects/` folder

## Optional:
You can also add:
- `profile-image.jpg` in the `public/` folder (your profile photo)
- `Resume.pdf` in the `public/` folder (your resume file)

Once you add these images, your portfolio will display beautiful previews of all your projects!
